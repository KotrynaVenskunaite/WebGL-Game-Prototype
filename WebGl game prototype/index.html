HOW TO PLAY - Use WASD to walk and QE keys to look around style="margin-top: 1%;"- Walk up to a character and press F to begin a lesson - Click the dialogue options to progress the game - After finishing a lesson, you can move on to the next one - There are 5 total lessons
CHARACTER MODELS ARE BASED ON: - "Vinny" is based on "Chamber" from "VALORANT" by "Riot Games" - "Choso" is based on "Choso" from "Jujutsu Kaisen" by "Gege Akutami" - "Vergil" is based on "Vergil" from "Devil May Cry" by "CAPCOM" - "Doc" is based on "Il Dottore/Zandik" from "Genshin Impact" by "MiHoYo"
MODEL AND TEXTURE SOURCES: - Lamp - Book - Plastic Chair - Helmet model is taken from "Dark Souls" by "FROMSOFTWARE" - Gojo Statue image - Messmer GIF
AUTHOR'S' COMMENTS: - You can ask a character to repeat a dialogue many times, pay attention to this if you are spamming skips - Try "Demo.Set_Dither_Shader_Variabled" in the console (yes, include the typo).
